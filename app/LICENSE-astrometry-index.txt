Astrometry.net index files (astrometry-index)

These are the index files the plate solver (solve-field) matches star
patterns against, made by the Astrometry.net team with its
build-astrometry-index program and downloaded from data.astrometry.net
(4100 series) and portal.nersc.gov (5200 LITE series). AstroFlow ships a
subset, for fields from about 20 arcminutes wide to the whole sky:

  index-4107 … index-4119   built from the Tycho-2 catalogue
  index-5203 … index-5206   built from Gaia DR2, with Tycho-2 for the bright stars

Astrometry.net: Lang, D., Hogg, D. W., Mierle, K., Blanton, M., & Roweis, S.
2010, AJ 139, 1782 (https://astrometry.net).
